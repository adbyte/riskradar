🌱 Sustainable Sourcing Report – 2023
Vendor Name: EcoGrow Inc.

EcoGrow Inc. is committed to ethical and sustainable raw material sourcing practices. As part of our sustainability goals:

Our wooden products are FSC-certified, ensuring responsible forest management.

We source Fairtrade-certified cotton for our apparel line to promote fair wages and labor rights.

All our food-grade packaging is made from USDA Organic certified paper materials.

Our coffee and tea products are sourced from farms that are Rainforest Alliance Certified.

Our textile division maintains compliance with the Global Organic Textile Standard (GOTS).
