💧 Water Stewardship Report — 2023
Vendor: AquaPure Solutions Ltd.

In 2023, AquaPure Solutions Ltd. consumed approximately 2.1 million gallons of water, across all facilities. We closely monitor our water usage through audited reports and smart metering tools.

Compared to 2022, we successfully reduced water consumption by 12%, thanks to our efficient systems and recycling technologies.

Our wastewater undergoes on-site biological treatment before being reused for irrigation and cooling processes. This initiative has allowed us to treat and reuse 100% of our wastewater.

We are proud to be certified by the Alliance for Water Stewardship since 2022, reinforcing our dedication to responsible water use and conservation.
